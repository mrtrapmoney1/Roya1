Platform Glossary & Quick Navigation

ServicePower

Primary service ticket management system. Used for scheduling, dispatching, and tracking all service calls from major appliance manufacturers and retailers.

New Leaf

Extended warranty service platform. Manages warranty claims, service requests, and customer information for extended protection plans.

Samsung GSPN

Samsung Global Service Partner Network portal. Used for Samsung-specific service tickets, parts ordering, and warranty claim submissions.

GE Parts Ordering

GE Appliances official parts ordering system. Order genuine GE replacement parts, check inventory availability, and track shipments.

Marcone

Multi-brand appliance parts supplier. Provides OEM and aftermarket parts for all major appliance brands with same-day pickup options at local branches.

Encompass Supply Chain

Comprehensive parts ordering platform for multiple appliance brands. Order parts, view schematics, check cross-references, and manage your account.
